---
name: learn-critique
description: Critique a draft subject map or concept curriculum for goal alignment, omissions, dependencies, and source coverage.
---

Read the `learn-session` skill’s `references/state.md` before reading or changing course records. Resolve that skill by its frontmatter name; installed folder names may differ. Reuse a contract already loaded in this session.

Read the brief, curriculum, relevant concept records, and supporting sources. Read `references/critic.md` from the resolved `learn-session` skill.

Check the artifact against the goal and external syllabus/source coverage. Look for missing outcomes, unnecessary depth, duplicates, prerequisite cycles or dangling links, scope mismatches, and unsupported teaching objectives. Judge the main-map stage at main-node granularity.

Write `curriculum-review.md`: reviewed revision and stage, overall readiness, and only actionable findings. Each finding names the affected node/objective, evidence, consequence, and smallest useful fix. Separate blockers from suggestions; accept an adequate plan without manufacturing criticism.

Return findings to the planner rather than changing the curriculum. After revisions, recheck affected findings and record the reviewed revision. A source gap blocks teaching the affected concept, not every sourced branch. The learner judges goal fit; technical coverage remains the critic’s responsibility.

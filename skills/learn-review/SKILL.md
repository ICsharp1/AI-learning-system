---
name: learn-review
description: Run due or requested concept reviews, assess delayed understanding, repair gaps, and update review dates.
---

Read the `learn-session` skill’s `references/state.md` before reading or changing course records. Resolve that skill by its frontmatter name; installed folder names may differ. Reuse a contract already loaded in this session.

Read due concept records, their objectives, prior errors, source references, and the review budget. Prioritize overdue reviews and consequential gaps; include a cross-concept question when it adds useful integration.

Start with unaided questions before explaining. Present one at a time and wait. Use stored prompts or verified fresh variants, with answer criteria hidden until the attempt. Record hints, source access, and any prior exposure; recognition of a repeated answer is weaker evidence than fresh application.

Give feedback after the attempt. Use `learn-teach` for targeted repair, then test a new related question; mark that success as post-repair, not as delayed retention. Leave untested objectives unchanged.

Use the shared scheduling rule and `learn-record` after assessed attempts. Update only concepts/objectives actually assessed. Keep questions, criteria, and history linked to stable IDs. Stop at the agreed budget or the learner’s request; leave remaining due items queued. Return a short account of retained ideas, gaps, and next reviews.

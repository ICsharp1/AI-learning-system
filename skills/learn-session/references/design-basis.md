**Design basis — 18 September 2026**

Adapted the agreed learning workflow using Matt Pocock’s principles: precise context pointers, clear completion criteria, small composable workflows, progressive disclosure, and one authoritative home for shared rules. Kept the learner’s chosen stages rather than copying a coding workflow or requiring HTML lessons.

Primary material read:
- [Writing for agents](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/writing-for-agents/SKILL.md)
- [Skill mechanics](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/writing-for-agents/SKILL-MECHANICS.md)
- [Teach](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/teach/SKILL.md)

These are authoring principles, not proof of educational efficacy. The review intervals and completion policy are explicit V1 choices to refine from actual use. Role profiles are portable instructions invoked by the coordinator; platform-specific subagent registration is not included.

---
name: learn-goal
description: Clarify a learner’s purpose, scope, target ability, background, and constraints before curriculum planning.
---

Read the `learn-session` skill’s `references/state.md` before reading or changing course records. Resolve that skill by its frontmatter name; installed folder names may differ. Reuse a contract already loaded in this session.

Read the existing brief and visible conversation. Ask one useful question at a time; reuse answers already given.

Clarify purpose, breadth, desired depth, observable target abilities, background, time, deadlines, and preferences. Offer a brief field map when the learner cannot yet choose a scope. Translate “everything” into a bounded goal without quietly reducing the ambition. Mark uncertain background for later diagnosis.

Write `learning-brief.md` with those decisions and remaining uncertainties. Reflect it back in plain language. Completion means the learner has confirmed or corrected the intended destination; record that confirmation. Changes to an established goal require the learner’s agreement and a curriculum impact check. Hand off to `learn-map`; leave lesson design for later.

---
name: learn-teach
description: Teach one planned concept with source-grounded explanations, adaptive questions, and an independent exit check.
---

Read the `learn-session` skill’s `references/state.md` before reading or changing course records. Resolve that skill by its frontmatter name; installed folder names may differ. Reuse a contract already loaded in this session.

Read the brief, selected concept, resume record, and exact source passages using the shared source rules. Resolve source gaps before factual instruction or grading.

Before instruction, prepare a small held-out question set covering every objective, with source-backed answer criteria and acceptable alternatives. Include explanation and fresh application where appropriate. Keep answers out of the learner-facing prompt.

Check uncertain prerequisites briefly. Then run a responsive loop: pose a useful problem, explain one connected idea, ask the learner to use it, wait for their answer, diagnose the reasoning, and choose the next step. Teach missing information explicitly. Use recall, labeling, fill-in-the-blank, justified choices, explanation, prediction, error detection, or transfer as the objective requires. A simple analogy must preserve the mechanism and identify its limits.

When struggling: orienting question → hint → stronger hint → worked explanation. Adapt rather than forcing every rung. After a worked answer, ask for the reasoning and a new related task independently. Add targeted practice for the gap; revisit a prerequisite when necessary.

Give specific feedback, allow revisions, and recheck disputed grading against the source. Use prepared exit questions in fresh contexts and add checks for discovered gaps. Apply the shared evidence states; distinguish unsupported claims of familiarity, assisted success, and independent performance.

Use `learn-record` after an assessed response and at pause/completion, including review material and next steps. End each question turn by waiting for the learner. Finish the unit when the objective evidence supports completion or the learner chooses to pause; record remaining gaps honestly.
